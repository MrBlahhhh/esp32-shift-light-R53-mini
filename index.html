Which board

Carrier board. The shift light as it ships: a 54 × 58 mm board with an ESP32-C3 SuperMini soldered in the middle, USB-C at the top. Plug the cable into the SuperMini. A bare SuperMini on the bench takes the same build.

Prototype. A Waveshare ESP32-S3-Zero wired to a CAN breakout and the strip. Plug into the S3-Zero's USB-C.

Either way the page reads the chip and picks the build itself. Flash it on the bench, disconnected from the car.

After flashing

  1. The board restarts on its own. Away from the car, the status LED blinks: on the carrier board the blue LED on the SuperMini blinks once a second, on the S3-Zero it blinks red. That's "no CAN", which is what it should say on the bench.
  2. Open the app and connect: the Android app, or logs.geekopolis.com/shiftlight in Chrome (Bluefy on an iPhone). There's no pairing and no PIN.
  3. After a fresh install it runs the default thresholds and colours. Set yours and press Save. An update keeps what was saved.

If it won't connect

  • Use a USB data cable. A lot of USB-C cables are charge-only and the board never shows up.
  • Close anything else holding the port: a serial monitor, PlatformIO, the Arduino IDE, another flasher tab.
  • Force the bootloader: hold BOOT, tap RST (RESET on the S3-Zero), let go of BOOT, then press Connect again. Plugging the cable in while holding BOOT does the same. On the carrier board both buttons are on the SuperMini, next to the USB-C.
  • No driver needed. Both boards use the chip's own USB. In the port list it's usually "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" or "USB Serial Device".
  • Chrome and Edge on a computer can do this. Firefox and Safari can't, and neither can a phone.

Source

The firmware is on GitHub: github.com/MrBlahhhh/esp32-shift-light-R53-mini. Each build links the commit it was built from.

The shift light itself, the housing and the assembled version: ryansdesigns.geekopolis.com/shiftlight.

Every file is checked against its sha256 before anything is written, and read back by md5 after.

Flashing uses Espressif's esptool-js (Apache-2.0).
